Our Framer template

Limonaia

Work/Limonaia

Framer template, real estate

The idea

Most property sites list floor areas. This one is meant to make people want to live there.

Limonaia is a Framer template for developers, agents and architects selling one new residential project. An arch opens on a hillside, the terraces drift past as you scroll, and the whole site moves like a slow film.

Launched 2026

What's inside

Seven page types, with 18 residences and 3 residence types on Framer's CMS. The list of homes filters, and every home gets its own page with a floor plan.

The hero has a morning and an evening. The lemon branches really move. On phones the pinned scenes have their own timing, so a flick of the thumb stays smooth.

It is on the Framer Marketplace. Swap in your photos and prices, and launch. The lemons are included.
