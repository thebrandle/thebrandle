The Brandle is a small design and development studio in Dubai, run by its three founders, with Ziyavdi on the edit. The people who design your project are the people who build it.

The team

Meet the humans

  • Muteeb Mehraj

    Founder / Chief of Design

    Dubai, UAE

    Muteeb founded The Brandle and leads design and build on every project: branding, UI/UX, websites and apps.

    If a logo here made you look twice, that was him. If a camel made you look three times, also him.

    He will happily talk you out of a feature you do not need. It is cheaper for everyone.

    LinkedIn

  • Raheem Dzhairkhanov

    Co-Founder / Chief of Media

    Dubai, UAE

    Raheem leads media at The Brandle. He is a videographer and a journalist, so he can film your launch and then interview it about what went wrong.

    Event films, founder interviews, behind-the-scenes content and venue work for cafes, bars and barbershops across Dubai. Every shoot is planned around what you actually need, then cut for social as well as the site.

    He has never said “we’ll fix it in post”. Out loud. If something good happens in Dubai and nobody filmed it, he takes it personally.

    Watch his films on our Work page.

    LinkedIn

  • Ubaid Wani

    Co-Founder / Chief of UI/UX & Product Design

    Srinagar, J&K

    Ubaid leads product and experience design at The Brandle. He takes a complicated problem and keeps going until it looks obvious, which is much harder than it sounds.

    Product strategy, UX, visual design and design systems: the whole process, from the first sticky note to the last pixel.

    If you ever used an app and never needed the help page, someone like Ubaid was quietly involved.

    LinkedIn

  • Ziyavdi Itayev

    Video Editor & Motion Designer

    Dubai, UAE

    Ziyavdi edits our videos and makes things move. Real estate, fintech, events, fashion, lifestyle: he has cut them all, from fast vertical Reels and TikToks to brand films, aftermovies, interviews and documentary-style stories.

    Pacing, story, sound design, colour and motion graphics. Hand him a detailed brief or a hard drive of raw footage and nothing else, and you get a platform-ready edit back, on a deadline that made the rest of us nervous.

    Ask him which frame was half a second too long. He knows. He always knows.

    Watch some of our edits.
