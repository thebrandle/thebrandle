Pricing and timelines

It depends on scope. A new brand identity plus a full custom site is a different project from a redesign or a landing page. Our fixed-price website plans run from $1,299 to $6,499 (about AED 4,800 to 23,900), and every project is scoped up front and quoted at a fixed price before work begins.

B

Yes. Our plans run from $1,299 to $6,499 (about AED 4,800 to 23,900). Basic and Pro cost about what a template build does in Dubai, and Max sits at the low end of the custom-design range. Every plan is still custom-designed, and the price is agreed before work starts. No additions appear halfway through.

B

A custom marketing site usually runs about 3 to 6 weeks from kickoff to launch, and a custom Shopify store is similar. App and web application MVPs run to a few months, phased so you see working software early. The timeline is agreed before we start, and how quickly we get content and feedback affects it.

B

Two rounds of revisions are included at each stage of the project. If you want changes beyond that, or the scope itself changes, we quote it before doing the work. Nothing is billed without your approval first.
