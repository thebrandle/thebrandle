Three founders, zero middlemen

plus two camels Dubai-based

The Brandle is a design and development studio in Dubai, founded by Muteeb Mehraj. We make websites, stores, brands and apps, all in-house.

How we work

Three founders, one small studio, nobody in between.

The Brandle is founded and run by three people: Muteeb on design, Raheem behind the camera, Ubaid on product and UX, with Ziyavdi cutting the films. The work happens in-house, so the people who design your project are the people who build it. You get one point of contact, a weekly call, and no chain of strangers passing your feedback along like a rumour.

Why like this

Fewer nasty surprises, on purpose.

You get a written scope and a fixed quote before any work starts, so the invoice matches the number you agreed to. A site or a store takes three to six weeks. When it is done, the code and the files are yours and we hand over the keys. If you stay with us after that, it is because you want to. We like it better that way.

The team

Meet the herd

If a logo here made you look twice, that was him. If a camel made you look three times, also him.

LinkedIn

Event films, founder interviews, behind-the-scenes content and venue work for cafes, bars and barbershops across Dubai. Every shoot is planned around what you actually need, then cut for social as well as the site.

He has never said “we’ll fix it in post”. Out loud. If something good happens in Dubai and nobody filmed it, he takes it personally.

LinkedIn

Ubaid leads product and experience design at The Brandle. He takes a complicated problem and keeps going until it looks obvious, which is much harder than it sounds.

Product strategy, UX, visual design and design systems: the whole process, from the first sticky note to the last pixel.

If you ever used an app and never needed the help page, someone like Ubaid was quietly involved.

LinkedIn

Ziyavdi edits our videos and makes things move. Real estate, fintech, events, fashion, lifestyle: he has cut them all, from fast vertical Reels and TikToks to brand films, aftermovies, interviews and documentary-style stories.

Let’s cook it

House rules 01

The quote is the quote

You get a written scope and a fixed price before any work starts. Anything extra gets quoted before we touch it. Invoices should be boring.

House rules 02

It’s all yours

On final payment you get the code, the design files and the logo files. You are never locked in to us, which keeps us on our best behaviour.

House rules 03

Funny is allowed here

A logo, a site or a film can do its job and still make someone smile. If a joke only works on other designers, it goes. The camels stay, obviously.

House rules 04

Simple beats clever

If a logo needs explaining or a website needs a manual, we got it wrong. We keep cutting until the thing is obvious, and we stop before anyone suggests a carousel.
