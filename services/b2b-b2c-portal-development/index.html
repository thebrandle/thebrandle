The honest bit

Portal or shop?

A shop sells to anyone at one price. A portal sells to people you already know, on terms you’ve already agreed. If each customer has their own price list, a normal shop will fight you.

Good fit if you are

  • A wholesaler taking orders by email or WhatsApp
  • A brand with dealers or distributors
  • Selling at prices negotiated customer by customer
  • Retyping orders into an ERP every day

Maybe not, ifeveryone pays the same price. Then a normal store does the job for less: see ecommerce website design. If the portal is one part of something bigger, that’s web application development.
