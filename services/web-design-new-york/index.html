What you get

Built for the US

  • Sales tax, set up

    Sales tax varies by state and city, so the store calculates it from the delivery address. In New York City that’s 8.875% combined, and other states have their own rates.

  • US checkout

    Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal and pay later with Afterpay, Affirm or Klarna.

  • Accessibility

    New York sees a lot of website accessibility lawsuits, so contrast, keyboard use, headings and alt text are built in from day one.

  • Overnight progress

    You send feedback at the end of your day, and the changes are waiting when you wake up.

  • Fast in America

    Hosting and a CDN chosen for quick loading across the US, not just near us.

  • Ready for ads

    Analytics, conversion tracking and clean product data in place before you spend on ads.

The honest bit

Why a studio nine hours away?

Because a time difference can work for you. Your evening feedback becomes our morning, so progress lands while you sleep. Calls happen in your early morning. If you need someone across the table in Manhattan, we’re not that, and we’ll say so first.

Good fit if you are

  • A New York founder happy to work async
  • A brand that wants senior design without Manhattan overheads
  • A store expanding sales across several states
  • Anyone who prefers a fixed price to an hourly rate

Maybe not, ifyou need daily calls in your afternoon. The time difference makes that hard, and we’d rather tell you now. For UK work see web design London. For product design, read UI and UX design.
